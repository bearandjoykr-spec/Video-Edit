# New reel prompt (copy, fill in, paste into a new session opened in the Video-Edit folder)

```
New Bear & Joy Instagram reel — make it as good as Day 23.

Footage: [file name in Downloads / Footage For Claude, or a Google Drive link]
Day number: DAY [24]
Topic in one line: [e.g. "why reading aloud matters more than flashcards"]

Korean translations (use word for word):
[English line]
[Korean line]
...

Key moments I want to pop: [e.g. "자신감", the joke at 0:40, the 3 tips]
Funny bit to punch in on (if any): [what you say and roughly when]

Follow CLAUDE.md and brand/BRAND.md exactly: the Day 23 v3 style (Pretendard, pastel
yellow/blue, word-by-word captions, pops with emoji and flags, 2–3 words behind my
head, punch zooms, DAY badge, progress bar with the bear, lo-fi music turned up,
5-second follow CTA). Korean captions max ~6 characters/sec, at least 1.2 s on screen.

Make a short version (≈30–35 s) first. Show me a storyboard before exporting,
then give me the Instagram package: MP4, cover and a Korean caption with 5 hashtags.
```

## Filming tips
- Open with a question parents worry about ("한국 부모들은 왜…?").
- One clear point per video, plus a short list if you have one (lists become great pop-ups).
- Include one funny or surprising moment (Day 23: "오른쪽? 왼쪽? 배고파요?").
- End on a line that ties to the brand ("…and that's the first little step").
- Send Korean translations up front.
- Good light, head and shoulders clear (needed for words behind the head), clip-on mic.
