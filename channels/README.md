# Channels (separate from Bear & Joy)

Each folder here holds the brand notes for a channel that is NOT The Bear & Joy Co.
`brand/BRAND.md`, the Bear & Joy logo, colours and end cards do not apply to these
channels unless the owner says so.

- Brand notes: `channels/<channel-name>/CHANNEL.md`
- Videos: `projects/<yyyy-mm>-<channel>-<topic>/`
