## Context
We start where God starts: **Genesis**. The name means "origin" or "beginning."

Jewish and Christian tradition holds that Moses wrote it, around three and a half thousand years ago, for a people who had just walked out of slavery in Egypt. Think about that. Egypt worshipped the sun, the river, the animals. And here comes a book that says the sun, the river and the animals are all just *made things*, and that one God made them all.

These opening verses answer the biggest questions a man can ask. Where did everything come from? Who am I? What am I for?

Today we read the opening of the story: the whole universe in six days, and the seventh day of rest. Next time we'll zoom in on one man, one garden, and the first command.

So settle in. Get your Bible open if you've got one. Let's read.

## Analysis
[Relaxed, conversational.]

Three things to notice.

**1. God speaks, and it happens.** Ten times in that chapter: *"And God said."* No battle between gods, which is how other ancient creation stories went. One word, and the universe obeys. That same God is the one whose Word we're reading.

**2. Order out of chaos.** It starts *"without form, and void."* Days one to three, God makes the *spaces*: light, sky and sea, land. Days four to six, he *fills* them: sun and stars, fish and birds, animals and man. Form, then fullness. God builds with order.

**3. Man is different.** Everything else is made *"after his kind."* Only man is made *"in the image of God."* Ancient kings set up statues of themselves to mark their territory. God put living images of himself into the world. That's what you are.

[PAUSE]

## For the brothers
[Lower, more direct.]

Now, brothers. What does this mean for a man of the Order today?

**Know whose image you bear.** The world will tell you who you are by your bank balance, your body, your follower count. Genesis tells you first: you were made in the image of God. You didn't earn it, and no one can take it. A knight didn't get his worth from his armour. He got it from the King he served. So do you.

**Dominion is stewardship, not domination.** God gave man authority, but authority *under* God, for the good of what's in his care. A real man doesn't use strength to take. He uses it to build and protect. Strength under control. That's the knight's code.

**Work hard, then rest.** God worked six days, and on the seventh he rested. Not because he was tired, but to set the pattern. Some of you never stop grinding. Some of you never start. Work with everything you've got, then lay down your tools and rest in God. Rest isn't weakness. It's trust.
