「AI 시대, 질문하는 아이」 — English narration for Mark (24 Nov 2026)
Calm, warm, a little storytelling. Short pause (·) where the picture changes. About 40 seconds in all.

[0–3 s · kids raise their hands]
In a world where AI can tell us everything…
what do our children really need?

[3–8 s · the AI chat answers]
Ask AI anything, and the answer comes back in seconds.

[8–13 s · book, search, asking someone are crossed out]
No need to find a book. No need to search. No need to ask anyone.

[13–17 s · the big question mark and the bubbles]
But what matters most isn't the answer.
It's the question.

[17–22 s · the classroom]
When we read a picture book together and ask, "What do you think?"…

[22–27 s · hands up, heads full of ideas]
…their little minds fill up with questions and imagination.

[27–34 s · Joy takes off the AI glasses]
And when they meet people from all over the world,
they can say what they think — in their own words.

[34–38 s · message card]
In a world where AI gives the answers,
what our children need… is the power to think.
Don't stop exploring. Don't stop looking for the answer.

[38–42 s · end card]
So, in the age of AI, what kind of education do our children need?
