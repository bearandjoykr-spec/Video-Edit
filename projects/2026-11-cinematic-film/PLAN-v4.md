# Brand film v4 proposal — "Born in Oxford · Built in Seoul" (18 Nov 2026, awaiting Joy)

Why v3 missed: it was a travel montage of stock footage. Nothing in it was ours until the last 10 s, and it had no
voice. Joy's reference (our own Book One read-aloud, youtu.be/0kBxYyic-4s, unlisted, Mark/M. Foster reading) shows
what *is* ours: Mark's storyteller voice, the watercolour bear, Oxford, and the 24-book journey.

## Concept
A 75–90 s brand film told the way our books are told: Mark reads it like a bedtime story (English), with Korean
subtitles. Structure = the tagline.

| Part | Picture | Voice (Mark, EN) / subtitle (KO) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Born in Oxford (0–20 s) | Joy's real Oxford footage (Radcliffe Camera, rooftops, Bridge of Sighs, butterfly mural) with the bear illustration fading in; book page art | "Once upon a time, in a lovely city called Oxford, there lived a small bear with no name." (already in the read-aloud) |
| 2. Built in Seoul (20–38 s) | Joy's Seoul footage (palace, hanbok, drum dance), a Korean family reading at home | "Then he crossed the world… to Seoul, where children were waiting to meet him." / Joy, KO: 「Bear가 Joy를 찾은 이야기!」 |
| 3. The journey (38–60 s) | The 24 cards turn like pages: Paris swan, Cairo camel, Chengdu panda, Tokyo crane… | "Twenty-four books. Twenty-four places. One little bear, looking for his name." |
| 4. Why (60–75 s) | Children with the book; Mark & Joy | "Children should meet English through beautiful stories, and the people they love." (from our own YouTube description) → KO: 영어는 외우는 게 아니라, 이야기와 사랑하는 사람을 통해 만나는 것 |
| 5. The hook + end (75–90 s) | Our book on Oxford stone (j6) → end card | "And at the very end… it's your child who gives him his name." → **Born in Oxford · Built in Seoul** |

Music: gentle piano (v3 candidates). Type: small storybook subtitles (v3 styles).

## Needed from Joy / Mark
1. The original read-aloud video file (for Mark's voice and the page art), or Mark records the script above (phone memo is fine).
2. The 24 card images (originals) and Book One page images or the PDF.
