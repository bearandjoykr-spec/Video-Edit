3 · Captions and hook

Korean first, English underneath

  • Two linesKorean caption in Gowun Batang Bold and Cream. When the spoken line includes an English book phrase, it appears below in gold Playfair italic. This bilingual line is something only Bear & Joy would do.
  • ChunkOne short phrase at a time (about 6–10 Korean syllables). Parents can follow at reading pace, with no single-word flashing.
  • HighlightAt most one gold word per caption, on the word that carries the meaning (그림책, 자신감, Joy).
  • PlaceCentred, top edge at 63% of the frame height, inside Instagram's safe zone (dashed box).
  • HookFirst 2 s: a small gold "BEAR & JOY · LITTLE STEPS" line, a big English title, and the Korean title under it. The same layout as the website hero.

4 · Cards and motion

Night-sky cards that turn like pages

  • CardsNight navy at 86% with a thin gold border and 24 px corners, sitting in the top third. Book pages and watercolour art go inside cards with a cream mat.
  • Step labelA gold outline pill in Playfair caps, e.g. "DAY 1" or "1일차". This matches Little Steps' day-by-day format.
  • ProgressA row of small stars at the top-left, one per step. A star turns gold when its step is done.
  • MotionCards fade and rise 16 px over 400 ms with a gentle ease. Book spreads change with a soft page turn or a 10-frame dissolve. No hard slides or glitch effects.
  • ZoomsSoft framing changes at each jump cut (100% to 105%). Slow push-ins on illustrations, like a camera drifting over a picture book.
  • ArtThe bear and the butterfly can appear in corners or on transitions, using your own artwork files.

5 · Pacing and sound

Calm, but never still

The reference changes the screen about every 10 s. Bear & Joy keeps that slower rhythm because parents are listening, not skimming. Long pauses and repeated takes are still cut so it never drags. This example shows a 60 s reel.

0s15304560
  • CutsRemove pauses longer than 0.4 s and repeated takes. Keep natural breaths so you sound warm, not rushed.
  • MusicSoft piano, celesta or acoustic guitar at 70–90 BPM, like a bedtime story. Mixed about 20 dB under the voice. Royalty-free or Instagram's library only.
  • Sound effectsA paper page turn when book art appears and a tiny chime when a star turns gold. Nothing else.
  • LoudnessFinal mix at −14 LUFS with peaks no higher than −1 dBTP.
